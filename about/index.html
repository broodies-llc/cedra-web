About us

We're there from the first measurement to the last hinge.

Assembled and installed on site · One of us on every job · English & Español

Cedra was founded by Victor and Renato, partners who have been assembling and installing cabinetry since 2020, mostly in new homes built by Florida home builders. Today, Cedra brings that pace and that standard to your home: kitchens, bathrooms, bars, closets, laundry rooms and more.

We design with you and source the materials you choose. Then we measure, assemble every cabinet on site and install it, with one of us there on every job.

Countertops are made by a specialist supplier: we can coordinate and install them for you, or work alongside the one you choose and check their measurements against our cabinets.

When you text us, one of us answers, in English or Spanish.
