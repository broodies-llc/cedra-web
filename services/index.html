Services

Cabinetry for every room you want to change.

We design with you, source the materials, assemble on site and install. One of us is there on every job.

Kitchen

  • Kitchens

    New cabinets for the whole kitchen or just part of it, designed around how you cook and store.

  • Islands

    A new island or a fresh look for the one you have: storage, seating, even two levels.

  • Countertops

    We coordinate your countertops with a specialist fabricator and install them, measured against your new cabinets.

Bath

  • Bathroom vanities

    Vanities and storage for bathrooms of any size.

Living

  • Bars & built-ins

    Bars, coffee stations and built-in storage.

  • TV walls

    Cabinetry and paneling around your TV, with storage and room to keep cables out of sight.

  • Accent walls & moldings

    Any design: wainscoting, board and batten, slat walls, crown molding and more.

Storage

  • Closets

    Closet systems and built-in storage, designed for your space.

  • Mudrooms

    Lockers, benches and storage for the entry that takes the daily traffic.

  • Laundry rooms

    Cabinets and counters to fold, store and keep laundry out of sight.

Many cabinet lines: plywood or particleboard, framed or frameless, soft-close available, and custom.

We can remove and haul away your old cabinets, or you can take them out yourself to save.

Appliances, faucets and light fixtures are supplied by others.
