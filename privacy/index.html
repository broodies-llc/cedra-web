Privacy

Cedra Cabinetry is a trade name of Pastorena LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

This site doesn't ask for personal information. It has no forms, no cookies and no analytics, and it doesn't load anything from other sites.

The site is hosted by GitHub, which records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as most web hosts do; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

To reach us, send a text message or WhatsApp to (407) 813-4664 or (407) 561-0050. We use your number and messages only to reply to you and to handle your visit or project, and we don't send marketing messages unless you agree to them in writing.

Photos you send us. You can send photos of your space by text message to either of our numbers. If you send them by WhatsApp, the same rules apply. These numbers are the personal phones of Cedra's founders, Victor and Renato, until Cedra has its own line. We use your photos only to prepare your design concept, including with an AI design tool. Please send only photos of the space that you took yourself — no people, documents or personal items. We delete your photos from our message threads when we deliver your concept. Copies that our phone and messaging providers keep in backups are deleted on those providers' own schedules. If you hire us, we keep your photos with your project records.
