How it works

Measure, assemble, install.

The same kitchen from the first cabinet to the last hinge, by one crew.

  1. 1Measure & start

    Every wall measured first. The pantry goes in on day one and sets the line for the rest.

  2. 2Assemble

    Our crew assembles the cabinets on site and sets uppers and bases to those measurements, in the finish you picked from real samples.

  3. 3Install

    We level and adjust every door and drawer before we hand the kitchen over.

HOW IT WORKS

Text us photos of the space you want to change

  1. Text photos to (407) 813-4664: one facing each wall you want to change, far enough back to see the whole wall from floor to ceiling. Lights on, phone held sideways.
  2. Add one from a corner that shows the whole space. Please send only photos you took, with no people, documents or personal items.
  3. Tell us your name, your ZIP code and, if you know them, the ceiling height (8, 9 or 10 ft, or "not sure") and the rough length of the longest wall, in feet or inches. For a kitchen, add the shape (one wall, L-shape, U-shape or galley) and whether there is an island. We estimate measurements from your photos and confirm them at the measure visit.
  4. Tell us what you want to change: kitchen cabinets (all or some), an island, a bathroom vanity, a bar or built-in, a TV wall, a closet, an accent wall or moldings. "Not sure yet" is fine too.
  5. We'll text you within 24 hours. Your concept arrives within 7 days, often sooner.

Your concept is free for requests through December 31, 2026. Starting January 1, 2027, it costs US$199, and the full amount is credited toward the first payment on your project if you sign your scope of work within 30 days after you receive your concept. It includes measuring, samples, design ideas, a render with up to 5 revisions, a materials estimate and list, a 2D plan and help choosing materials.

Your concept is a preliminary design by our team, created with the help of AI rendering from your photos. Colors, finishes and proportions are approximate, and it is not a photo of completed work. Final measurements, materials and price are set in the scope of work you sign.

Write in English or Spanish, whichever you prefer.

This site doesn't upload or store photos. You send them by text message to one of our numbers, which for now are the personal phones of Cedra's founders. If you send them by WhatsApp instead, the same rules apply. We use them only to prepare your concept, including with an AI design tool, and delete them from our message threads when we deliver it; if you hire us, we keep them with your project records. Please send only photos of the space that you took yourself — no people, documents or personal items. See our Privacy Policy.
