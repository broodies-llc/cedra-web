FAQ

Questions we hear most.

How long does it take?

Once your payment is confirmed, we usually place your order the next business day. In-stock cabinet lines usually arrive in about 7 to 10 days, and installation usually takes up to 3 days. Countertops are usually installed 1 to 3 days after the cabinets. Custom orders take longer; we confirm the timeline at your measure visit.

How does payment work?

In three parts, under a scope of work we both sign: 50% when you sign it, 30% on the day we start the work, and 20% two to three days after we finish. If you paid for a design concept, that amount is credited toward the first 50%. You have 3 business days after signing to cancel and get back everything you paid when you signed, and we place your order after that period. We accept cash, Zelle and bank transfer, and you get a written receipt for every payment.

Is the measure visit free?

Yes, for visits requested through December 31, 2026, with no obligation to hire us. Starting January 1, 2027, the measure visit is part of the design concept (US$199).

Is the design concept free?

Yes, for concepts requested through December 31, 2026. Starting January 1, 2027, it costs US$199, and the full amount is credited toward the first payment on your project if you sign your scope of work within 30 days after you receive your concept. It includes measuring, samples, design ideas, a render with up to 5 revisions, a materials estimate and list, a 2D plan and help choosing materials. The render is a preliminary image made with the help of AI.

Do I need to be home during the installation?

Only if you want to. We agree on access with you before we start.

Do you remove my old cabinets?

If you'd like. We can remove and haul them away, or you can take them out yourself to save.

Can I use my own countertop supplier?

Yes. We work alongside them and check their measurements against our cabinets.

Can you match or add to my existing cabinets?

Yes. At the measure visit we look at what you have and find the closest option.

Do you do custom cabinets?

Yes. Custom orders take longer; we confirm the timeline at your measure visit.

Who reconnects the sink and appliances?

We install cabinets and countertops. Plumbing and electrical work, such as connecting or moving a sink, cooktop or dishwasher, must be done by a licensed plumber or electrician. We can recommend one, you hire them directly, and we coordinate schedules so the work fits together.

Do you work in my area?

We work in Central Florida. Text us your ZIP code and we'll let you know. Service area

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Write to us in English or Spanish, whichever you prefer.
